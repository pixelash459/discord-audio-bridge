# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to people of every background and experience level.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- When you disagree, argue the substance. "This will click at buffer boundaries because…" is
  useful; "this is terrible" is not.
- Remember that most contributors are volunteers spending their own time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and
whenever someone is representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through
[GitHub Security Advisories](../../security/advisories/new) or by opening a private contact with a
maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may temporarily or
permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
